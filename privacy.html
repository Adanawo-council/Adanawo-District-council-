Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Adanawo District Council ("we") runs this website for the people of Adanawo District. This page explains what we keep about you and why.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your name and email address. We do not receive your Google password.
  • What you choose to add: your picture, village, a few words about yourself, and your WhatsApp number.
  • What you post: pitches, comments, likes, market items and suggestions.

How we use it

  • To show your name and profile on the website, as you set it up.
  • To let you post, comment and sell.
  • Your WhatsApp number is visible only to the palace administrators, and to buyers if you put it on a market item.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not send advertising.

Where it is kept

Your information is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and the website is hosted by Cloudflare.

Your choices

You can change your profile at any time under "My profile". To have your information removed, message us on WhatsApp using the "Report any technical issue" button on the Home page.

Contact

Use the "Report any technical issue" button on the Home page of this website.
